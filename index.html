Moonlit River Between the Peaks

The boat drifted quietly, its wooden frame sighing against the gentle pull of the river. The water was dark, yet alive with silver threads where the moonlight touched. On either side, mountains rose like solemn giants, their peaks softened by mist, their shadows stretching long across the current. The oars rested, forgotten, as the boat surrendered to the rhythm of the stream.


Above, the moon hung low, a pale lantern watching over the journey. Its reflection trembled on the surface, breaking and reforming with each ripple. The air carried the cool breath of the mountains, scented faintly with pine and stone. Silence was not empty here—it was full, layered with the hum of water, the whisper of wind, the distant cry of unseen creatures.


The boat seemed small, yet perfectly placed, as though it belonged to this night. Each moment stretched, timeless, as the river curved and carried it deeper into shadow. The mountains leaned closer, their ridges glowing faintly under the moon’s silver gaze. The world felt hushed, suspended between water and sky.


And so the boat moved on, steady and unhurried, beneath the watchful moon and the towering mountains, carried by a river that sang softly of forever.
